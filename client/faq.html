Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ceramic craft, shipping guarantees, and daily care.

How are fragile ceramic pieces packed for transit? +
Every ceramic dish, mug, and dinner plate is encased in custom molded high-density foam, double-walled corrugated cartons, and sealed with tamper-proof reinforced tape. In the rare event of transit damage, notify Clayvistaindia@gmail.com within 48 hours for an instant, no-questions-asked replacement.
Are ClayVista ceramics microwave and dishwasher safe? +
Yes! All of our stoneware and porcelain tableware lines are high-fired at 1280°C, fully vitrified, and 100% microwave and dishwasher safe. The only exception is items with hand-applied 24K real gold leaf lustre (such as our Imperial Bone China line), which should not be placed in a microwave.
What is your free shipping policy? +
We provide complimentary insured express courier delivery on all orders above ₹1,999 across all Indian pin codes. For orders under ₹1,999, a nominal fragile handling and freight fee of ₹150 is applied.
Are your glazes certified food-safe and lead-free? +
Absolutely. All ClayVista raw materials and mineral glazes are independently tested and certified 100% free of lead, cadmium, and arsenic. Our tableware complies with both Indian FSSAI and international US FDA food contact regulations.
Do you offer bespoke hospitality and wholesale orders? +
Yes! We partner with boutique cafes, 5-star hotels, luxury villas, and corporate gifting houses. Please visit our Contact page or WhatsApp our concierge at +91 81786 64347 for wholesale catalogues and tiered volume pricing.
Can I cancel or modify my order after placing it? +
Orders can be cancelled directly through your User Dashboard while the status remains "Placed". Once our studio artisans have inspected and packed your shipment for courier pickup, cancellations are subject to our standard return policy.

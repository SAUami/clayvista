Our Heritage & Mission

Honoring the Tactile Alchemy of Earth, Water, & Fire

Founded with a commitment to bridge centuries of traditional Indian pottery guilds with modern Scandinavian and Japanese dining aesthetics, ClayVista brings warmth, mindfulness, and timeless dignity back to the shared table.

The Genesis

From Khurja & Jaipur to Your Dining Room

In an era of mass-produced plastic tableware and machine-stamped ceramics that chip at the slightest touch, ClayVista was created to restore intentionality. We partnered with generational artisan clusters across Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan to preserve traditional wheel-throwing techniques while innovating high-temperature electric kilns.

Today, our tableware graces private residences across 40+ Indian cities, Michelin-starred bistros, and bespoke luxury weddings worldwide.

How We Craft

The 5 Stages of ClayVista Ceramics

Every piece journeys through 18 days of patient artisanal shaping, air-drying, and high-heat transformation.

01

Clay Sourcing & Wedging

Natural organic stoneware and bone ash clays are mixed with filtered water and hand-wedged to expel micro air bubbles.

02

Wheel Throwing & Carving

Turned individually on the potter's wheel. Rims and foot rings are trimmed by hand using brass looping tools.

03

Bisque Firing (900°C)

The dried greenware undergoes its preliminary firing to harden the clay matrix while leaving it porous for mineral glazes.

04

Mineral Glazing & Gold

Dipped in custom non-toxic mineral suspensions. For our royal lines, 24K real gold leaf is hand-burnished along the rim.

05

Vitrification at 1280°C

The ultimate high-heat fusion melts silica into glass, permanently sealing pores for chip-proof, microwave-safe daily use.
